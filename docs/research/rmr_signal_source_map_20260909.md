- **Isolated-shock recoil V1**: only 23 eligible first-shock events across both indices. STAR50 had a descriptive pooled recoil tendency driven mainly by UP shocks, but support was tiny and direction/year inconsistent; CSI1000 had only 4 events and no recoil. Closed under the frozen first-shock identity.
- **Lunch-boundary normalization V1**: 1,304 eligible trading days were measured, but material lunch displacements (`Z>1`) were only 40 STAR50 and 5 CSI1000 cases. STAR50 pooled 5m signed path remained slightly continuation-biased (`+0.8951 bp`, reversal probability `47.5%`); CSI1000 had only five material events. Frozen support/annual/directional gates failed. Therefore market-closure normalization does not generalize from Overnight to the lunch break under this identity.

## Working synthesis

The strongest surviving sources all contain an explicit **normal-state anchor + temporary displacement**:

- Overnight: market-closure/opening boundary supplies the anchor;
- R1: intact parent trend supplies the anchor;
- R2: intact parent range supplies the anchor.

By contrast, naked residual dislocation, naked overshoot, high activity around a displacement, isolated shock magnitude, and lunch-boundary displacement did not produce robust standalone RMR evidence.

This is a working research hypothesis, not a theorem: **future broad discovery should search for distinct causal structural anchors before inventing new extreme scores.**
